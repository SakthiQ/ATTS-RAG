# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a vice president. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Paid time off

Paid time off (PTO) accrues according to length of service, as shown below.

| Years of service | Annual PTO |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 years | 15 days |
| 3 to 5 years | 20 days |
| 6 or more years | 25 days |

Unused PTO may be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may be taken at any time within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Expenses and travel

Meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day while traveling. Receipts are required for every expense above $25. Flights and hotels must be booked through the travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences. Conference attendance requires manager approval.
