# Acme Corp Q3 2026 Results Summary

## Financial highlights

Total Q3 revenue was $95.2 million, up 7.8% from Q2. Operating margin was 18.4%. Net revenue retention was 112%.

## People and customers

Headcount at the end of Q3 was 1,240 employees, up from 1,185 at the end of Q2. Customer churn fell to 4.2% from 5.1% in Q2. The net promoter score (NPS) was 41. Acme served 3,310 enterprise customers.

## Outlook

Management expects Q4 revenue between $98 million and $101 million, driven by the launch of the Atlas analytics product in November. The company plans to open a new office in Singapore in early 2027.
