# Acme Atlas Support Plans (2026)

## Plans and response times

Atlas support is offered in three plans, as shown below.

| Plan | Seats included | Monthly price per seat | First response time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 1 to 10 seats | $12 | 24 hours |
| Business | 11 to 100 seats | $9 | 4 hours |
| Enterprise | 101 or more seats | $7 | 30 minutes |

Annual billing gives a 15% discount on every plan. Prices exclude tax.

## Service hours and uptime

Basic and Business support is available Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 UTC. Enterprise support is available around the clock, every day of the year. Atlas is committed to 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below 99.9%, customers receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25%.

## Cancellations and refunds

Customers may cancel at any time. Refunds are available within 30 days of the first purchase only. Data is kept for 60 days after cancellation and then deleted permanently. Customers can export their data at any time using the admin console.
