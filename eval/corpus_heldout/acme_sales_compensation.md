# Acme Sales Compensation Plan (FY2026)

## Commission rates

Sales representatives earn commission on closed deals according to the annual quota attainment tiers below.

| Quota attainment | Commission rate |
| --- | --- |
| Below 50% | 2% |
| 50% to 99% | 5% |
| 100% to 149% | 8% |
| 150% or more | 12% |

The annual quota for a sales representative is $800,000. Commission is paid monthly, in the month after the deal is closed.

## Bonuses and accelerators

Representatives who close a deal with a new enterprise customer receive a one-time bonus of $2,500. The President's Club trip is awarded to the top 10 representatives by quota attainment. Commission on multi-year contracts is paid only on the value of the first year.

## Clawbacks

If a customer cancels within 90 days of signing, the commission paid on that deal is reclaimed. Deals closed in the final week of the fiscal year are reviewed by the finance team before payment.
